FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about my Google Ads, Meta Ads, and performance marketing services.

What is the minimum advertising budget?

The ideal budget depends on your business goals, industry, target market, and competition. For initial testing, a monthly budget of $500–$1,000 can be a reasonable starting point for some businesses. I'll recommend a suitable budget after understanding your business and campaign objectives.

How long does it take to see results?

Initial performance data can typically emerge within the first 2–4 weeks. However, meaningful optimization and more consistent results may take 2–3 months, depending on the campaign, industry, budget, and conversion cycle.

Do you guarantee specific results or ROAS?

No ethical advertising professional can guarantee a specific ROAS, sales volume, or lead count. I focus on strategic campaign management, accurate tracking, continuous optimization, and transparent reporting to improve performance based on real data.

Will I own my advertising accounts?

Absolutely. Your Google Ads, Meta Business, and related advertising accounts remain under your ownership. I work through authorized access, ensuring you maintain full control of your business assets and campaign data.

Which advertising platforms do you work with?

I specialize in Google Ads and Meta Ads, including Facebook and Instagram advertising. My services also cover campaign tracking, conversion measurement, audience targeting, and performance optimization.

Do you provide conversion tracking setup?

Yes. I can help configure and verify Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Tag Manager (GTM), Google Ads conversion tracking, Meta Pixel, and Conversions API (CAPI), depending on your website and business requirements.

Do you work with both new and existing ad accounts?

Yes. I can help businesses launch campaigns from scratch or audit and optimize existing advertising accounts. The approach depends on your current setup, campaign history, and business objectives.

Do you provide regular performance reports?

Yes. I provide clear performance updates and reporting to help you understand campaign results, advertising spend, conversions, and optimization opportunities. Reporting frequency can be agreed upon based on the project scope.
